Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Draft for the early-access site. This page is plain-language and has not yet had legal review. It will be updated before public launch.

The short version

This early-access site collects very little. We do not sell your information.

What we collect

  • Email address, only if you choose to join the early list through the signup form.
  • Basic technical data that any web host records, such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested. This is used to keep the site running and secure.

How we use it

We use your email to send updates about IncidentAxis, such as launch news and early-access offers. You can ask to be removed at any time by emailing the address below.

Who handles it

The site is hosted by Netlify, which also receives and stores signups from the early-list form on our behalf. We may later move the list to an email-newsletter provider, and will only use it for IncidentAxis updates.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not run advertising trackers on this site.

Your choices

You can ask us to access or delete your information, or to remove you from the list, by emailing the address below. California residents may have additional rights under California privacy law, and we will honor valid requests.

Children

This site is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy as the product develops. The date above shows the latest version.
