Built by Marcel Cafferata, California fire engineer

I've spent 25 years in the fire service. I built IncidentAxis so you can practice running the first minutes of an incident out loud, before the real call.

How a Test run works

  1. 01 / 30 seconds

    Study the scene

    Look over the arrival view before the call comes in. What is exposed, and who is at risk? Plan your strategy and tactics.

  2. 02 / Dispatch

    Hear the dispatch

    A short call: the type of incident, the location, and one thing a caller could see.

  3. 03 / Command

    Take command

    Give your size-up and orders out loud, with no buttons to tap. You get 2 minutes on Static scenarios and 6 minutes on Dynamic ones, where the incident answers as you go.

  4. 04 / After

    Get your result

    See what you did well, what to work on, and one thing to try next time. Only you see it.

Learn mode

Starts with the dispatch, with no study timer. Watch and listen to a model size-up for the scene, then compare it with your own.

Test mode

Command the scene yourself. Your orders are checked against a grading rubric.
