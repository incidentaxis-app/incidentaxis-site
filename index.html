Built by a working firefighter

I'm Marcel Cafferata, a California fire engineer with 25 years in the service. I built IncidentAxis so you can practice running the first two minutes of an incident out loud, before the real call.

How a run works

  1. 01 / Start

    Hear the dispatch

    A short call: the type of incident, the location, and one thing a caller could see.

  2. 02 / 30 seconds

    Study the scene

    Look over the arrival view before you speak. What is exposed, and who is at risk?

  3. 03 / 120 seconds

    Take command

    Give your size-up and orders out loud, with no buttons to tap. The incident answers as you go.

  4. 04 / After

    Get your result

    See what you did well, what to work on, and one thing to try next time. Only you see it.

Learn mode

Watch and listen to a model size-up for the scene, then compare it with your own.

Test mode

Command the scene yourself. Your orders are checked against a grading rubric.
